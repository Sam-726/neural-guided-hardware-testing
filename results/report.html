How to interpret this

Higher functional-bin counts indicate more diversity under this hand-written metric. They are not HDL branch coverage or a percentage of all reachable states. The four bugs are intentionally seeded; no real processor vulnerability was discovered. A neural method is not assumed to beat the heuristic.

Reproducibility

All strategies share the same seeded candidate pools and 32-test warm-up. Guided selectors have 15% random exploration. Only executed tests contribute to coverage. The neural model trains online on novelty, never mutation mismatches. Simulator and model overhead are included in run times; initialization is excluded.
